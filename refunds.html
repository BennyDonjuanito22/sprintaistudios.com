Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: February 11, 2026

Last Updated: February 11, 2026

Sprint AI Studios is a trade name operated by ASI Innovative Solutions LLC. All refunds are processed by ASI Innovative Solutions LLC.

1. Service-Based Refunds

As a professional services company, refunds are handled differently than product sales. This policy covers project cancellations and refund eligibility.

2. Project Cancellation by Client

2.1 Before Work Begins

Timing Refund
Within 24 hours of deposit Full refund of deposit
After 24 hours, before work starts 75% refund of deposit

2.2 After Work Has Begun

Project Stage Refund Calculation
0-25% complete Refund unused portion minus 25% of project value
26-50% complete Refund unused portion minus 50% of project value
51-75% complete Refund unused portion minus 75% of project value
Over 75% complete No refund; remaining balance may still be due

3. Project Cancellation by Us

If we must cancel a project:

  • Full refund for any undelivered work
  • Delivery of any completed portions
  • 30 days notice when possible

4. Retainer Services

4.1 Monthly Retainers

  • Cancellation: 30 days written notice required
  • Unused hours: Do not roll over or refund unless otherwise agreed
  • Early termination: Current month is non-refundable

5. Hourly Projects

  • Billed for time worked
  • Unused hours from prepaid blocks may be refundable
  • Minimum billing increments apply (typically 15-30 minutes)

6. Deliverables Upon Cancellation

When a project is cancelled:

  • You receive work completed up to cancellation date
  • Deliverables provided after final payment for completed work
  • Work-in-progress may be provided at our discretion

7. Disputes & Satisfaction Issues

If you're unsatisfied with our work:

  1. Contact us immediately to discuss concerns
  2. We'll work to address issues within scope
  3. Additional revisions may be offered
  4. Partial refunds considered case-by-case

8. Non-Refundable Items

  • Third-party costs (software licenses, hosting, etc.)
  • Rush fees for expedited delivery
  • Completed and approved milestones
  • Consultation fees (unless otherwise agreed)

9. How to Request Cancellation/Refund

  1. Email: hello@sprintaistudios.com
  2. Subject: "Project Cancellation - [Project Name]"
  3. Include: Reason for cancellation, project details
  4. Response: Within 1-2 business days

10. Refund Processing

  • Refunds processed within 7-10 business days of approval
  • Refunded to original payment method when possible
  • Check or wire transfer for large refunds
